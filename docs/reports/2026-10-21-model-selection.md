## Selection decision

**Override: `hgb` selected in place of `logreg`.**

The automatic rule picked `logreg` (within 1 SE of `hgb`, simpler). That rule is overridden for the following reason: `logreg` breaks in the 2009 and 2010 folds. The Platt calibrator fits a negative slope (`a < 0`) in those folds because logistic regression extrapolates linearly into a regime (the 2008 financial crisis) it has never seen in training, producing inverted scores. This is visible in the calibration diagnostic (Platt `a` = −1.37 in the 2009 fold). As a result, `logreg` fires an alert in only 3 of 37 weeks of the 2008 episode (#2, the largest in the development window), compared to `hgb`'s 22. `hgb` is better on every secondary metric: row F1 (0.429 vs 0.257), PR AUC (0.571 vs 0.366), Brier (0.148 vs 0.192), false-alarm runs (6 total vs 11), and predict-head F1 (0.221 vs 0.172). The `logreg` 2009/2010 failures are not random variation — they reflect a structural property of linear classifiers when the training set has no historical analogue to the test regime.

**`hgb` vs VIX level:** event F1 0.527 vs 0.511; paired-difference CI straddles zero (not statistically distinguishable). `hgb` is on par with the VIX level rule, not better. This is the expected bar for a model trained on the same underlying stress signals.

**Committed before confirmation run:** this decision is recorded prior to opening the 2018–2021 held-out window.
